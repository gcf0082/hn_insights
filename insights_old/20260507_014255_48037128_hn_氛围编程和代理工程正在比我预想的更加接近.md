**发布日期**: 2026年5月7日  
**讨论主题**: Vibe coding and agentic engineering are getting closer than I'd like  
**来源**: Hacker News

## 核心观点总结

### 1. LLM并未创造混乱，而是加速暴露了既有问题

Vibe Coding和大语言模型并没有造就缺乏纪律的工程组织或工程师。它们只是**暴露并加速**了这些问题。之前就存在缺乏标准和实践的工程师和团队，现在他们能够更快捷地生产更多代码。

> " Plenty of engineers have loose (or no!) standards and practices over how they write code. This concept isn't new, it's just a lot easier for individuals and teams who have never really adhered to any sort of standards in their SDLC to produce a lot more code."

### 2. 工程师的本质上不会改变

糟糕的工程师继续糟糕，优秀的工程师继续优秀。优秀的工程师并非因为写得快而优秀，而是凭借**经验、审慎思考**和分享关键洞察来引导系统方向。

> "The best engineers I know were ones who drew on experience and careful consideration and shared critical insights with their team."

### 3. 优秀工程师的特质：慢而稳定

优秀工程师反而**更慢**，在生产环境故障时**不那么恐慌**，能够逐步推理解决任何问题。而急于求成的开发者往往会犯更多错误，使情况更糟。

> "The ones that I'd call 'good engineers' were slower, less panicked when production was down and could reason their way (slowly) through pretty much anything."

### 4. 职场对工程本质的误解

许多公司忽视这一点，将工程师视为**流水线工人**，认为完成10个任务的人比完成5个的更优秀。

> "Unfortunately, a lot of workplaces are ignoring this, believing their engineers are assembly line workers."

### 5. LLM与乱码代码的局限性

大模型能够处理各种代码乱码，但会引入额外的回归错误，遗漏更多需求。代码只是表面上看起来正确，但存在微妙bug，更危险。

> "They can kinda work with whatever pile of code you give them... to a point. So it manifests as the LLM introducing extra regressions, or dropping more requirements than it used to."

### 6. 对未来代码质量的担忧

有人担忧30年后，整个行业将是数十亿行LLM生成的代码构成的一团糟，几乎没有人类读过任何代码，最终将无法维护。

> "People in the future are going to wonder what the hell we were thinking, when 30 years down the line everything is a hot mess of billions of lines of code generated by LLMs that no human has read."

### 7. LLM改进放缓的潜在风险

如果LLM停止改进，它们仍会生成数十亿行连自己都无法推理的代码，导致质量下降。

> "If LLMs stop improving at the pace of the last few years (I believe they already are slowing down) then they will still manage to crank out billions lines of code which they themselves won't be able to grep and reason through."

### 8. 正确使用LLM的方法

LLM代码可以通过投入时间进行**训练、提示、验证和审查**来减少乱码。生成完美遵循SOLID原则、包含单元测试且无警告的代码从未如此简单。

> "LLM code can be less of a hot mess than human written code if you put in the time to train/prompt/verify/review."

### 9. 核心问题：不爱惜代码的人获得了强力工具

> "The only people who are going to put in the time, are people who care enough to. The problem is you have people who didn't care before who were equipped with a garden hose. Now that they have a fully pressurized fire hose they can make more of a mess faster."

### 10. 微妙bug比明显错误更危险

看起来正确但包含微妙bug的代码比明显错误的代码**更危险**，因为它更难被审查发现。

> "If the code doesn't compile, that's easy to spot. If the code compiles but doesn't work, that's still somewhat easy to spot. If the code compiles and works, but it does the wrong thing in some edge case... that's harder to spot."

### 11. 最终责任仍在人类工程师

无论使用什么工具，**责任最终由人类工程师承担**。用户不会接受"我使用了AI"的借口。

> "If I get pwned because my AI agent wrote code that had a security vulnerability, none of my users are going to accept the excuse that I used AI. I will get the blame."

### 12. 软件开发生命周期已过时

整个软件开发生命周期是围绕**每天几百行代码**的生产速度设计的，而现在完全不同了。

> "The entire software development lifecycle was, it turns out, designed around the idea that it takes a day to produce a few hundred lines of code. And now it doesn't."

### 13. 代码行数的意义

代码行数(LOC)作为度量虽然不好，但可以用于衡量**可理解性**，审核2000行与200行的工作量差异巨大。

> "LOC is useful here not because it's a metric for output but because it's a metric for understandability. Reviewing 200 lines is a very different workload than reviewing 2000."

## 讨论要点分析

这场讨论反映了业界对AI辅助编程的双重担忧：

1. **质量风险**：当AI生成代码的速度远超人类审核能力时，技术债务累积
2. **技能退化**：过度依赖AI可能导致工程师失去深度思考和推理能力
3. **责任真空**：当代码由AI生成时，谁来承担最终责任？
4. **反馈循环**：如果下一代LLM在质量低下的AI生成代码上训练，模型质量会持续下降
